# Sample picture conversation

This example is the conversation array consumed by the picture-conversation
feedback feature. The conversation activity owns how responses are captured.

## Scene

Picture: children flying a red kite while families have a picnic in a park.

## Conversation

**Tutor:** Describe what is happening in the picture.

**Learner:** Two children are flying a red kite in the park. Families are having
a picnic nearby.

**Tutor:** Have you flown a kite with someone before? What did you enjoy about it?

**Learner:** I flew a kite with my sister at the beach because we liked running
together in the wind.

## Example learning map

**Strengths**

- The learner clearly described people and actions in the scene.
- The learner answered the follow-up with a reason and a personal example.

**Next focus**

`track: grammar` · `skill: articles`

The learner could practise adding articles consistently, for example: “The
children are playing football in a park.” The lesson feature chooses the mission
title and exercise for this skill.

## Refinement example

**Tutor:** Describe what is happening in the picture.

**Learner:** Children football.

**Tutor:** Tell me a little more. Who can you see, and what is happening?

The feedback feature still shows the available results. It marks unsupported
areas as `not_assessed`, explains what could help it understand more, and lets
the learner choose whether to keep talking about the picture.
