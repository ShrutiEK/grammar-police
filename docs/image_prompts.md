# Picture-description image prompts

These prompts are designed for the Grammar Police speaking assessment. Each scene gives beginners easy objects and actions to name while giving advanced learners relationships, causes, emotions, and possible stories to explain.

## Shared generation instructions

Add the following direction to every prompt so the set feels consistent:

> Create a child-friendly, detailed editorial illustration in a warm, colourful, semi-realistic storybook style. Use a landscape 4:3 composition at 1200 × 900 pixels. Show the full scene clearly with distinct foreground, middle-ground, and background activity. Include diverse people with natural expressions and realistic proportions. Use bright daylight, clean shapes, and enough visual detail for a 30–60 second spoken description. No written words, labels, logos, watermarks, speech bubbles, frightening content, dangerous behaviour, or identifiable brands.

## 1. A busy neighbourhood park

A lively public park on a sunny afternoon. In the foreground, two children fly a red kite while their dog pulls gently at its lead. Nearby, a family shares fruit on a picnic blanket and a toddler reaches for a rolling orange. In the middle-ground, children play football, an older woman waters flowers, and a boy helps his friend after a bicycle chain slips off. In the background, people jog along a path, ducks swim across a pond, a gardener pushes a wheelbarrow, and dark clouds begin gathering behind the trees.

## 2. The morning market

An open-air community market filled with colourful fruit, vegetables, flowers, baskets, and cloth canopies. A vendor weighs tomatoes for a customer, a child points at a large watermelon, and another shopper compares two bunches of bananas. A delivery worker carries a crate, a cat watches a fish stall from a safe distance, and a few loose apples have rolled beneath a table. In the background, people greet one another, a cyclist waits beside the entrance, and birds sit along a rooftop.

## 3. The school science fair

A cheerful school hall during a science fair with no readable signs. Small groups of students demonstrate a model volcano, a solar-system display, a simple robot, a seed-growing experiment, and a paper bridge. One volcano has just released harmless colourful foam, surprising the nearby children. A teacher crouches to examine the bridge, parents take pictures, one student adjusts a crooked planet, and another child comforts a nervous presenter. Tables contain magnifying glasses, leaves, magnets, gears, and notebooks without visible writing.

## 4. A railway station reunion

A bright, clean railway platform where a train has just arrived. A child runs toward a grandparent with open arms while another family unloads suitcases from a carriage. A porter pushes a luggage trolley, a passenger checks an analogue clock, and a woman helps an older man step down safely. One small suitcase has tipped over and a scarf trails from it. In the distance, travellers wait on benches, pigeons peck near a water fountain, and green hills are visible beyond the station roof. Do not include readable station names.

## 5. The beach clean-up

A wide seaside scene during a friendly community clean-up. Children and adults wearing gloves collect bottles and wrappers into separate coloured bags while leaving shells and seaweed untouched. One child uses a litter picker, two friends carry a heavy bag together, and a volunteer helps a crab move away from the busy area. Other families build a sandcastle, a lifeguard watches the water, surfers carry boards, and a fishing boat passes in the background. Wind lifts a sunhat while gulls circle above.

## 6. A rainy city crossing

A bustling city street during gentle rain. At a zebra crossing, a schoolchild shares an umbrella with an older neighbour while a traffic officer helps pedestrians cross. A cyclist wears a helmet and raincoat, a bus waits behind the line, and a delivery rider secures a box. One person steps around a puddle while another child happily jumps into it. Reflections glow on the wet road, people shelter beneath shop awnings with no signs, and a rainbow is beginning to appear between distant buildings.

## 7. The family kitchen challenge

A spacious home kitchen where three generations prepare a colourful meal together. A grandparent rolls dough, an adult stirs a pot, one child washes vegetables, and another carefully decorates small cakes. Flour has spilled beside a mixing bowl, a curious cat watches a dangling towel, and a timer is about to ring. The table holds carrots, tomatoes, herbs, bread, bowls, and measuring cups. Through the window, another family member can be seen setting an outdoor table in the garden.

## 8. A day at the zoo

A spacious, ethical wildlife park with natural habitats and safe viewing areas. In the foreground, a keeper feeds leaves to a giraffe while children watch quietly with an adult. Nearby, monkeys climb ropes, an elephant sprays water, flamingos stand on one leg, and penguins dive into a clear pool. One child looks through binoculars in the wrong direction and a sibling points toward a peacock opening its tail. Show shade, water, plants, and plenty of space for every animal; no cages or animal performances.

## 9. The library surprise

A welcoming community library with tall shelves, reading corners, plants, and large windows. A librarian helps a child find a book, two friends build a tower from large picture books, and a younger child listens to an adult reading aloud. Another student reaches for a book using a small step stool while a classmate steadies it. A pile of returned books leans slightly on a trolley, sunlight forms patterns on the floor, and outside the window a mobile library van arrives. All book covers are illustrated but contain no readable text.

## 10. The village festival

A joyful village festival in a large open square decorated with bunting, lanterns, flowers, and handmade crafts, but no readable banners. Musicians play drums and flutes while children and adults dance in a circle. A potter shapes clay, a craftsperson weaves a basket, families share snacks, and two children try to carry an oversized paper decoration. A garland has fallen near a goat that is sniffing it. In the background, neighbours hang more lanterns and the evening sky begins to turn orange.

## 11. The community garden

A flourishing community garden divided into small vegetable and flower beds. Children plant seedlings, an older gardener demonstrates how to use a watering can, and two friends compare a tiny carrot with a very large pumpkin. A wheelbarrow holds compost, bees visit sunflowers, a scarecrow wears a crooked hat, and a rabbit peeks from behind a fence without entering the beds. In the background, rainwater is collected in a barrel, people share harvested vegetables, and apartment balconies overlook the garden.

## 12. The airport departure hall

A bright airport terminal with travellers preparing for different journeys. A family organizes suitcases, a child watches aeroplanes through a huge window, and an employee helps a passenger using a wheelchair. A pilot and cabin crew walk past, a cleaner cares for the floor, and a security worker returns a forgotten teddy bear to a relieved child. One luggage trolley rolls slightly away while its owner catches it. Show clocks and simple symbols only, with no readable destination boards, airline names, or logos.

## 13. Camping beside the lake

A family-friendly campsite beside a calm lake at sunset. One group puts up a tent, another safely cooks vegetables at a supervised camp stove, and two children collect fallen twigs without harming plants. A child studies a map with a compass, someone hangs a lantern, and another person rescues a hat drifting near the shore with a long stick. Canoes return to a small jetty, birds fly over the water, mountains reflect in the lake, and a deer watches from far across the clearing.

## 14. The sports day mix-up

A school sports field during a friendly sports day. One race is finishing while other children take part in a sack race, relay race, long jump, and beanbag toss. A runner has accidentally crossed the finish line holding the relay baton, making teammates laugh kindly. A teacher checks a stopwatch, volunteers offer water, classmates cheer, and one child ties a friend's shoelace before the next event. Include cones, hoops, flags, and a scoreboard with shapes rather than readable numbers or words.

## 15. The museum comes alive

A bright natural-history museum gallery where families explore large exhibits. Children examine a dinosaur skeleton, colourful minerals, old pottery, insects, and a model of an ancient boat. One child copies a dinosaur pose, another peers through a magnifying glass, and a guide demonstrates how a simple fossil formed using sand and shells. A dropped brochure with no readable text lies near a bench while its owner looks for it. Through an archway, a giant whale model hangs above another gallery.

## 16. A busy hospital courtyard

A calm, welcoming hospital courtyard focused on everyday care rather than illness. A nurse helps a child adjust a small arm sling, a doctor speaks reassuringly with a family, and a physiotherapist guides an older person using parallel rails. Other visitors sit beneath trees, volunteers distribute water, and children paint a cheerful mural with no words. A gardener trims a hedge, a therapy dog greets someone gently, and an ambulance is parked quietly in the distant background with no emergency occurring.

## 17. The riverside rescue drill

A supervised community safety practice beside a calm river. Rescue workers demonstrate life jackets and throw a flotation ring toward a training dummy while families watch from behind a safe barrier. One child checks that a friend's jacket is fastened, another points to a small boat approaching the jetty, and volunteers organize ropes and first-aid supplies. Ducks swim away from the activity, a fisherman packs up his equipment, and clouds suggest that light rain may arrive. Make it clearly safe, controlled, and non-frightening.

## 18. The repair café

A friendly community workshop where people repair household objects instead of throwing them away. At separate tables, adults and children fix a bicycle, sew a torn backpack, glue a wooden chair, and examine a silent table fan with its power disconnected. A child sorts buttons by colour, a volunteer finds the correct spanner, and another person celebrates as an old lamp lights up safely. Shelves hold labelled-by-picture tool boxes, spare parts, fabric, and jars, with no readable words or brand logos.

## 19. A snowy town morning

A lively town square after fresh snowfall. Children build a snow family, neighbours clear a path together, and a shopkeeper places seed for birds. Someone helps push a small car gently from soft snow while another person pulls groceries on a sled. A dog wears one missing boot and looks back toward it lying near a bench. In the background, people skate on a supervised rink, smoke rises from chimneys, snow rests on trees, and warm light shines through windows without visible shop names.

## 20. The floating lantern evening

A peaceful lakeside celebration at twilight using reusable floating lanterns collected by volunteers. Families decorate lanterns with patterns but no writing, musicians perform softly, and children help place lights along a path. One lantern has tipped sideways on the grass and two friends work together to straighten it. Food stalls serve colourful snacks, grandparents tell a story to a small group, a photographer frames the sunset, and volunteers in a boat gather every lantern from the water. Stars and a crescent moon appear above distant hills.

## Generation and selection checklist

- Generate at least three variations of each prompt and choose the clearest composition.
- Verify that hands, faces, objects, and spatial relationships look natural.
- Reject images containing accidental writing, logos, visual clutter that hides actions, or unsafe behaviour.
- Keep a consistent illustration style, colour palette, aspect ratio, and approximate camera distance across all 20 images.
- Save optimized web versions with stable names such as `picture-prompt-01.webp` through `picture-prompt-20.webp`.
- Write factual alt text and a private reference description for each accepted image; assessment should compare the learner's statements against that reference description.
